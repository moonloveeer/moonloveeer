Quantum Resistant Ledger (QRL) is a proof-of-work blockchain purpose-built to stay secure in a post-quantum world. Accounts rely on XMSS hash-based signatures so signatures cannot be forged, even by future quantum computers.

This devnet instance showcases simplified mining, configurable halving schedules, and a lightweight mempool so builders can experiment with QRL economics, block rewards, and validator tooling. All wallet access flows through Web3 signatures—no passwords to steal or brute force.

The dashboard combines current chain health with hardening features you can trust: Web3-only authentication, CSRF safeguards, strict Content Security Policy, and rate-limited mining endpoints. Connect a wallet, mine blocks, and watch supply growth and rewards update in real time.
